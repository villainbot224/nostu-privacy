Nostu for iOS

Privacy Policy

Version 1.3Effective 11 September 2026

Nostu gives real places a shared timeline. It is built so that most of what it knows never leaves your iPhone.

  • Nostu runs no servers. There is no Nostu database, anywhere, holding your data.
  • What you publish for everyone goes to Apple's CloudKit, in Nostu's iCloud container — not to a machine we own.
  • Nothing you keep private ever leaves your device.
  • There are no third-party SDKs in Nostu: no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporters, no trackers.
  • Nostu never sells or shares personal information, and never builds an advertising profile of you.

Who is responsible

Nostu is developed and published by Visakh V Nair, an individual developer, who is the data controller for the purposes of the GDPR and equivalent laws. Nostu is not yet operated by an incorporated company; if that changes, this page will be updated and the version number raised before the change takes effect.

This policy covers the Nostu iOS app and its Home Screen widget. It does not cover Apple's own services — iCloud, Sign in with Apple, Apple Maps and the App Store are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

What Nostu collects, and where it lives

Everything Nostu holds is in one of three places. The complete, itemised list is inside the app at Settings › Privacy Center › Data Dashboard — and it is the same list the app's own deletion code walks, so the two cannot drift apart.

WhereWhat
On your iPhone Your memories and the places they belong to; private and contributors-only memories in full; your profile and settings; private voice notes; achievements; safety counters; the usage record described below.
In iCloud Only memories you publish for everyone, and the places they attach to. Stored through Apple's CloudKit in the container iCloud.com.nostu.app. Your block list is stored separately, in your own private iCloud database.
Never stored Your movements; text read out of your photos during safety checks; face counts; anything Nostu computes in the moment and discards.

There is no fourth place. Nostu operates no server, no API and no database of its own, and the app contains no code that sends your data to any third party.

What becomes public when you publish

A memory is uploaded only when you have set it to Everyone and it has passed the on-device safety checks. Anything you keep private, or restrict, stays on your device and is never uploaded.

When you do publish, this is what travels with it, and it is visible to other people using Nostu:

  • The photograph, or the voice recording, and its duration.
  • Your title, your note, and the mood you chose.
  • Your first name, shown as the author. This is the one piece of your identity that becomes public. You can change it in the app, and it is the only name Nostu ever publishes — never your full name, never your email address, never your Apple Account.
  • When it was captured, which way you were facing, and the altitude.
  • The tags Nostu's on-device image recognition produced for the photo.
  • The place: its name, its city and country, and its coordinates — because a shared timeline is meaningless without the place it belongs to.

Comments you leave on public timelines carry your first name and your words, and are visible in the same way.

A published memory is a chapter in a place's story. You can delete your own chapters at any time, and Nostu removes them from iCloud. Other people's chapters in the same timeline are theirs, and stay.

Location

Nostu asks for location while using the app only. It is used for exactly two things:

  • At the moment you capture a memory, to work out which real place you are standing at.
  • While the map is open, to show what is near you.

Nostu may also ask for temporary precise location at capture time, so that it can tell one building from the one next door and your memory joins the right timeline rather than the shop next door's.

Nostu keeps no history of your movements. There is no background location, no significant-location monitoring, no geofencing, and no record of where you travelled between places. Your position is used in the moment and discarded. Coordinates persist only as the coordinates of a place, and only when you publish a chapter to it.

Photos, audio and metadata

Every photograph — whether you take it in Nostu or choose it from your library — is decoded, resized and re-encoded before Nostu stores it. A practical consequence of that: the JPEG Nostu keeps and publishes is built from pixels alone, so the original EXIF metadata does not survive it. No embedded GPS coordinates, no camera serial number, no lens or exposure data travels with your photo.

If you turn it on, Nostu can also save a copy of a shot you took into your own Photos library. That copy is yours, in your library, and Nostu does not read the rest of it.

Private voice notes you record for yourself are audio files on your iPhone. They are excluded from device backups, never uploaded, never analysed, and never audible to anyone else. Deleting the memory deletes the recording.

On-device safety checks

Before a photograph can be published to a shared timeline, Nostu checks it. Every one of those checks runs on your iPhone, using Apple's own frameworks. Nothing is uploaded for analysis, and no image is ever sent anywhere to be reviewed.

The checks look for adult content, violence, child-safety concerns, screenshots, advertising, duplicates, and photographs that do not appear to match the place you are at. Nostu reads text visible in the photo in order to spot advertising and screenshots — that text is used for the decision and thrown away. It is never stored, never indexed, never uploaded.

What is kept is the result: whether the photo passed. It is stored with the memory on your device, so a chapter's right to be public is a recorded fact rather than something re-decided later.

Nostu also keeps a private score on your device, used only to decide which photograph represents a place on the map. It is never shown to you or anyone else, it is never uploaded, and it can never hide or delete a memory.

If a check cannot run, your memory is still saved to your device — it simply is not published until it can be reviewed.

Faces

Nostu counts faces in a photograph to warn you when it is mostly a portrait, or when a crowd is visible — because the people in your photograph did not agree to anything.

It detects rectangles, and only rectangles. There is no face recognition, no identification, and no face template of any kind is ever created, stored or transmitted. The count exists for a fraction of a second and is discarded.

Reports and blocking

When you report something

A report is delivered to a write-only drop box in Nostu's iCloud container. It carries the category you chose and an identifier for the content — never the image itself, never your words, and never your location. CloudKit stamps the report with an account identifier assigned by Apple, which is how reports are grouped and rate-limited.

That record type grants no read access to anyone. The person you reported cannot fetch it, and no user can enumerate reports. Only Nostu's operator can read reports, and only for moderation.

When you block someone

Your block list is stored in your own private iCloud database, so it follows you to your other devices. It is scoped to your Apple Account: Nostu cannot read it, no other user can read it, and it is never disclosed to the person you blocked.

Safety counters

Nostu keeps a few private counters on your device: how many chapters you have published, how many uploads were refused, timers that slow runaway posting, and a record of any warning or restriction on your account so that you can see and appeal it. None of it syncs, none of it is shown to anyone, and its only effect is a very small adjustment to how carefully your next upload is checked. Negative marks fade automatically over about six weeks.

Product analytics

Nostu keeps a short record of things that happen while you use it — the app starting, a timeline opened, a memory read, a memory published, a share. It is how the developer can tell whether the app works.

  • It stays on your iPhone. Nostu has no analytics endpoint configured and no server to send it to. It has never left a device.
  • It notes that something happened and when — never what was in it. No photographs, no captions, no notes, no titles, no names.
  • It contains no coordinates and no record of where you travelled. A place appears only as a one-way fingerprint that cannot be turned back into its name or its position. City and country are kept in plain form.
  • It is attached to a random identifier for this installation, deliberately unrelated to your Apple Account.
  • Erasing your data erases it, identifier included.

If this ever changes — if a destination for this record is ever configured — this policy will say so, with a raised version number, before it happens.

Your account

Nostu uses Sign in with Apple. There are no Nostu passwords, and Nostu never sees your Apple Account password.

Apple gives Nostu a private identifier unique to this app. It is stored in your iPhone's Keychain and is what makes your account restore itself if you reinstall. Apple may also give Nostu your name and email address on the first sign-in — including a private relay address, if you choose to hide your email.

Your email address is stored only on your device, where it is shown back to you in Settings. It is never uploaded, never published and never used to contact you. Your first name is used as your author name, and only your first name is ever published.

Contacts and your profile picture

Your profile picture is one of two things: an illustrated face that Nostu draws, or the photograph from one of your own Nostu memories. Every account starts with the illustrated face.

You cannot upload a picture as your profile picture — not from your Photos library, not from the camera, and not from anywhere else. A photograph can only become your profile picture if it belongs to a memory you made in Nostu while signed in to your account; you choose it with Use as Profile Photo on that memory, or from Choose from My Memories. A memory made by someone else, or by a different Nostu account, can never be chosen.

Nostu keeps which memory you chose, not a separate copy of its photograph, and that choice belongs to your Nostu account. If the memory is deleted or can no longer be shown, your profile goes back to the illustrated face. It does the same when you sign out, and a different account signing in on the same iPhone never sees your choice.

Nostu does not use your contacts for your profile picture and never asks for Contacts permission. Your profile picture is not published to shared timelines and is not shown to other people using Nostu.

The Home Screen widget

Nostu's widget shows memories you already have. To do that, the app writes a small snapshot — a few lines of text and thumbnail images — into a private container shared between the app and the widget on your device.

That container is on your iPhone. Nothing in it is uploaded, and nothing in it is readable by any other app.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Nostu registers with Apple's Push Notification service so that iCloud can tell you when a timeline you contributed to has changed. Apple's push token is handled by Apple and CloudKit. Nostu operates no push server and holds no device tokens, because there is nowhere for it to hold them.

Sharing and diagnostics

When you tap Share, Nostu renders a card and hands it to the iOS share sheet, or to Instagram Stories if you choose that. What happens after that is governed by whichever app or service you send it to — not by this policy.

Nostu can also generate a diagnostic report you can send to the developer if something goes wrong. It describes the app's state, not your content, and it passes through a redaction step before it is written. It is only ever created and sent by you, deliberately — it is never generated in the background or transmitted automatically.

What Nostu never does

  • Nostu contains no third-party SDKs at all. No analytics library, no advertising library, no crash reporter, no attribution kit, no tracking pixel. Every framework it uses is Apple's own.
  • Nostu does not track you across apps or websites, and does not use the Advertising Identifier.
  • Nostu does not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone.
  • Nostu does not read your contacts, your browsing, your calendar, your health data or what else is on your device.
  • Nostu does not use your content to train machine-learning models.
  • Nostu shows no advertising.

Your rights and controls

Wherever you live, you can do all of this from inside the app, immediately, without asking anyone:

  • See everything stored — Settings › Privacy Center › Data Dashboard.
  • Export a copy — Privacy Center › Download My Data.
  • Clear individual categories — Privacy Center › Clear Local Data.
  • Delete a published chapter — open it and delete it. Its copy in iCloud is removed too, and it cannot come back.
  • Delete your account and everything with it — Privacy Center › Delete Account. This immediately and permanently erases your memories, your profile, your Sign in with Apple identifier, your safety data, your voice notes and your usage record from the device.

One limitation, stated plainly. Deleting your account erases everything on the device, but chapters you had already published to a shared timeline are not removed from iCloud by that step. If you want them gone as well, delete them individually before deleting your account — or write to the address below and they will be removed for you. This is a gap in the current version and it is being closed; when it is, this page will say so.

Under the GDPR and comparable laws you also have the right to rectification, restriction, objection, and to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Nostu is built so that access, portability and erasure need no request at all — but if you want any of these handled by a person, or need something the app cannot yet do itself, write to the address below and it will be actioned within one month.

Nostu has no legal basis to keep anything you have asked it to delete, and there is no backup system that would resurrect it.

Retention

  • Memories are kept until you delete them.
  • Rate-limit timers expire within a day.
  • Reports you have filed are kept for up to six months.
  • Account warnings, restrictions and appeals are kept for a year, so you can see and contest them.
  • When you delete a published memory, Nostu keeps only its identifier for a month, so that the copy in iCloud is removed and cannot reappear. That note holds no photograph, no words and no place.

Nothing is kept longer than it is useful.

Children

Nostu is not designed for children and is not in the App Store's Kids Category. It shows photographs of real public places, published by strangers. Nostu does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child is using Nostu, or that content on Nostu puts a child at risk, write to the address below and it will be acted on immediately.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in substance, the version number at the top of this page changes with it, and you will be told inside the app before the new version applies. Typo fixes do not raise the version.

A copy of this policy also ships inside the app, at Settings › Privacy Center, so you can read it without a network connection.
